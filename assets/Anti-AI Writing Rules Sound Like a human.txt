Anti-AI Writing Rules: Sound Like a Human
Voice and Stance
•  Take a position. Write like someone who believes something, even if it offends part of your audience.
•  Use "you" and "your" to speak directly to the reader.
•  Skip the setup. Never open with "In today's fast-paced world" or any scene-setting throat-clear.
•  Drop transition words like "furthermore," "moreover," and "additionally." Connect ideas with a period or a short new sentence instead.
Sentences and Rhythm
•  Mix your sentence lengths. Short. Then longer and more complex. Then short again. Break the medium-medium-medium pattern.
•  Use contractions. Write "don't," "you're," "it's." Full words in every sentence read like a legal document.
•  Allow fragments. They work. They create pace.
•  Avoid staccato stop-start sentences that feel choppy without purpose.
Specificity Over Polish
•  Name real places, real people, and real outcomes. "A 40% drop in churn at a 12-person SaaS company in Austin" beats "exceptional results" every time.
•  Replace hypothetical examples ("Imagine a founder who...") with actual stories, even if they're yours.
•  Drop buzzwords: "leverage," "delve," "unlock," "utilize," "harness," "skyrocket," and anything that sounds like a McKinsey slide.
Structure and Lists
•  Break the rule of three. Use two examples sometimes. Use five. The pattern itself is the tell.
•  Avoid the "not just X, but also Y" construction. It is a formula, and readers feel formulas.
•  Use bullet points when listing, but do not make every response a bulleted list.
Personality and Imperfection
•  Include at least one opinion, one personal detail, or one admission of uncertainty per piece.
•  Leave rough edges. Not every sentence needs to land perfectly.
•  Do not use em dashes anywhere. Use a period or a semicolon to connect ideas.
•  Avoid the words: can, may, just, that, very, really, literally, actually, certainly, probably, basically, could, maybe, delve, embark, enlightening, esteemed, shed light, craft, crafting, imagine, realm, game-changer, unlock, discover, skyrocket, abyss, not alone, in a world where, revolutionize, disruptive, utilize, utilizing, dive deep, tapestry, illuminate, unveil, pivotal, intricate, elucidate, hence, furthermore, realm, however, harness, exciting, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, remarkable, it, remains to be seen, glimpse into, navigating, landscape, stark, testament, in summary, in conclusion, moreover, boost, skyrocketing, opened up, powerful, inquiries, ever-evolving.
