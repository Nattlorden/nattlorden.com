[Neo-Verdi lyrical tenor scene, reflective and weary, intimate nocturnal atmosphere, restrained orchestral sorrow]

[Intro — Low strings, distant rain against stone]
Once, Camelot breathed like fire.
Now it breathes like hot ash:
still alive…
but tired.

[Verse 1 — reflective]
The halls are full every night.
Laughter.
Music.
Wine poured until dawn.

Yet I remember a time
when a glance was enough
to feel the heart of the kingdom beating.

Now everything continues…
without truly burning.

[Bridge — quiet melancholy]
Guinevere still smiles kindly.
I respond with the same sweetness learned over the years.
No open wounds.
No reproaches.

And that's
what scares me most.

[Chorus — warm but aching]
Because love doesn't always die in the fire.
Sometimes it slowly recedes,
like mist over the lake,
leaving the forms intact...
but emptying the warmth.

And we continue to live
between polite words,
between hands that still seek
without truly finding each other.

[Orchestral interlude — horn and distant oboe]

[Verse 2 — deeper loneliness]
Even Lancelot laughs less than he once did.

He still brings honor to the halls,
but something in his eyes
always looks elsewhere.

And Mordred…

my son…

often walks with Morgaine
near the old shores.

He speaks freely with her
as he no longer does with us.

When he looks at me,
I see respect.

Never intimacy.

As if I had become
a statue built to rule
and not a man to love.

[Bridge — introspective]
Perhaps this is the destiny of kingdoms:
to slowly transform men
into the symbols the people desire.

And the symbols...
can no longer touch each other.

[Final Chorus — broader, sorrowful restraint]
The halls of Camelot are too silent
behind all the festive music.
Too perfect.
Too orderly.

As if the entire kingdom
was holding its breath
waiting for something
that no one dares to name.

And I...
who wanted peace above all things...
begin to fear
the quiet we've built.

[Outro — soft, resigned]
The rain continues on the walls.
The torches tremble in the wind.
And Camelot seems to listen
to its own weariness.

[Soft unresolved ending]
