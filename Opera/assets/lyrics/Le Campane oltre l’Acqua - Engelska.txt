The bells... the bells...
beyond the water, beyond the wind,
who calls them in the night?
Who answers from the convent?

[Women's choir—softly, like voices in the fog]
The veil falls over the lake,
the island is silent in the blue.
Where the boats once sailed,
Avalon is no longer visible.

[Men's choir—grave, rising]
Crosses arise on the hills,
new altars, new laws.
Sword and fire along the borders,
peaceless kings, peoples and flocks.

[Full chorus—more agitated, restless]
A time of crowns comes,
a time of swords comes.
Every house listens and trembles,
every street calls to street.

Who has seen the white deer?
Who has heard the ancient horn?
Only the wind bears the name,
only the sea remains a friend.

[Orchestral rise — agitated strings, restrained brass, distant timpani]

[Women's choir — mournful, lyrical]
Mothers, sisters, daughters of the river,
guard the little light.
If the fog closes the heart,
who remembers the first name?

[Men's choir — marked, almost a sacred march]
They say: a promised king is coming.
They say: order will be born.
They say: heaven has chosen iron.
They say: Britain will live.

[Full chorus — great Verdian crescendo]
But beneath the bronze of the bells,
beneath the voice of the new gods,
something cries in the fountains,
something dies within us.

It is not a storm,
it is not a battle,
it is not just death or war.
It is the memory that grows thin,
it is the ancient blood of the earth.

[Bridge — darker, almost religious, choir in layered responses]
The bells call.
— Who answers?
The crosses rise.
— Who descends?
The kings fight.
— Who forgives?
The island flees.
— Who returns?

[Women's choir — suspended, pianissimo]
Avalon…
Avalon…
do not leave us without dreams,
do not return alone in song.

[Men's choir — sotto voce, fearful]
If the fog blocks the path,
if the shore loses its name,
what remains for the lonely man?
Bread, iron, law, and hunger.

[Full chorus — final statement, tragic and noble]
The bells beyond the water
bring no peace.
They bring the dawning day
and the silent night.

O Britain, divided land,
between the cross and the ancient altar,
who will save your wound?
Who will still remember?

[Outro — orchestra decresce, bells fading, female choir distant]
The veil falls over the lake,
the island is silent in the blue.
Where the boats went,
Avalon is no longer visible.

[A final distant bell, unresolved cadence]
