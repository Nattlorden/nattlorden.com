[Slow operatic lament choir, extremely restrained, neo-Verdi sacred atmosphere, no solo voice, dark romantic orchestra, distant bells, low strings, cold mist over water]

[Women's choir — pianissimo]
Under the veil of evening,
the nameless lake sleeps.
No oar breaks the water,
no fire calls home.

[Men's choir — very soft, grave]
Memory passes slowly
among the stones and the reeds.
Every year the song falls,
every winter takes voice.

[Mixed choir — sustained, almost whispered]
Avalon…
Avalon…

[Women's choir]
Who remembers the first oath?
Who guards the ancient fire?
The roads lead to kings,
but the mist takes them elsewhere.

[Low men's choir — deep and distant]
Under the earth the dragon sleeps.
Last breath of the night.
In the bones of the hill
the heat of the flame remains.

[Mixed choir — slow crescendo, still restrained]
Do not call him.
Do not wake him.
His name weighs like stone
in the blood of men.

[Women’s choir — fragile, suspended]
Merlin still listens to the sea.
Merlin still bears the mark.
But the waters block the passage,
but the stars forget.

[Men’s choir]
Every gift seeks an end.
Every flame seeks ash.
Even the most ancient guardian
enters softly into the silence.

[Mixed choir — fuller harmony, never loud]
Avalon…
does not disappear in the fire.
Avalon…
is lost in memory.

[Orchestra — low strings and distant horn only]

[Women’s choir — final invocation]
If the fog returns,
perhaps the song will return too.
If anyone remembers,
perhaps the lake will still have a name.

[Final chorus — fading]
Avalon…
Avalon…

[Long unresolved ending, distant bells, no climax]
