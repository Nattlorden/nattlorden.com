The torches slowly burn out.

Even Camelot
seems afraid of the silence
that is entering these rooms.

Arthur…

So finally fate
leaves us alone.

No advisors.
No knights.
No crowns.

Only I…
and my brother
at the end of the world.

Don't speak.
Don't waste the little breath that remains.

I brought something for the pain.

Ancient herbs.
Ancient waters.
The same that once
healed kings
before men forgot the earth.

Yes…

like this.

Now I see it.
The torment slowly leaves your eyes.

You finally rest.

God…
it's been years
since I've seen peace on your face.

Don't ask me for forgiveness.

Not today.

Because I will not deny Avalon.

I will not deny Britannia.

I will not deny what I tried to save.

But this…
this blood…
this ruin…

was not what I wished for you.

Between us two, Britannia is dead.

Between your dream of order
and my dream of mystery,
the earth has cracked
like ice beneath the sun.

And yet no man
has ever borne the weight of the crown
so gently.

No man
has ever loved his people
to the point of complete exhaustion.

Perhaps we were both too old.

You were born for a world
that men did not yet deserve.
I was born for one
that was already fading.

And Britannia…
poor Britannia…

has asked us to choose
between two halves of the same soul.

Look…

the rain stops.

Even the sky
seems tired of crying.

Rest now, Arthur.

Let others
build new wars,
new churches,
new lies.

You have already given enough blood
to this king-hungry land.

And if Avalon still exists
beyond fog and memory...

then perhaps one day
you'll finally understand
that I never stopped
loving you as a brother.

Camelot sleeps.
Britannia sleeps.

And in my hands
the king slowly lets
the world go.
